Core Principles

Understanding The Three Biological Forces

According to Ayurveda, health is the state of dynamic equilibrium between the three Doshas that govern all physiological processes.

Vata (Air & Space)

Governs all bodily movement, respiration, nerve impulses, and circulation. When aggravated, Vata leads to dryness, anxiety, insomnia, joint stiffness, and chronic body ache.

Balancing Therapies: Abhyanga with warm medicated oils, Kizhi, Sirovasti.

Pitta (Fire & Water)

Controls digestion, metabolic conversion, hormonal balance, vision, and body temperature. Excess Pitta causes inflammation, skin sensitivities, hyperacidity, and stress.

Balancing Therapies: Shirodhara, Tarpana eye therapy, cooling herbal infusions.

Kapha (Earth & Water)

Provides structural stability, lubrication to joints, tissue nourishment, and immunity. When out of balance, Kapha results in lethargy, congestion, sluggish metabolism, and weight retention.

Balancing Therapies: Swedam herbal steam, dry powder massages, Agni-stimulating choornams.
